FAQ

Fast answers for buyers.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. Tell us your size, colors, crystals, and whether it’s wall-mounted or tabletop.

Are stands/frames included?

No, unless the listing says so. Black iron stands/risers can be added.

What’s the typical turnaround time?

Usually 2–4 weeks depending on complexity and cure time.

How do I care for resin?

Avoid heat and harsh cleaners. Wipe with a soft cloth.
