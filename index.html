Meet the Artist

The Creative Force Behind High Maintenance Resin Art

Kat is the artist behind High Maintenance Resin Art, creating bold resin pieces inspired by crystals, geodes, shimmer, and dramatic layered finishes. Her work is built to stand out — combining glamour, texture, and one-of-a-kind movement in every pour.

Each piece is made by hand and shaped by the natural flow of resin, which means no two creations are ever identical. From smaller decorative work to larger custom statement pieces, the focus stays on originality, detail, and visual impact.

High Maintenance Resin Art is about more than just decor — it is about creating artwork with personality, shine, and a high-end feel that instantly changes the energy of a space.
